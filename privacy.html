Privacy

This site counts visits so the club can see what is useful. It does it without cookies and without keeping anything that identifies you.

What is counted

  • Which page was opened, and which site or link sent you (for example Instagram, or a link we tagged).
  • Whether you are on a phone, tablet or computer, and your browser's timezone (a rough region).
  • How long the page was in view, which parts of the page you stopped on, and which buttons and links you used (join links, calendar, email signup).
  • Whether this browser has visited before, using one small note kept in the browser itself.

What is not kept

  • Your IP address is never stored.
  • No cookies, and no advertising or third-party trackers.
  • A visitor is only a scrambled code made from a random daily value, your network address and your browser. The daily value is thrown away, so the code cannot be linked from one day to the next or turned back into you.

Who sees it

Club admins, and any editor an admin has chosen. Only totals and lists are shown, never a single person's history. Records older than 13 months are deleted automatically.

Do Not Track

Because nothing personal is kept, visits are counted even when your browser sends "Do Not Track" or "Global Privacy Control" (Brave and Firefox do by default).

Email updates

If you ask for email updates, we keep your address only to send them, after you confirm it. Every email has an unsubscribe link, and unsubscribing deletes the address.
